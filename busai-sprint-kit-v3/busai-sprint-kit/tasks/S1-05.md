## Context

The Warner SDK cannot set sub-stream resolution or fps, so G1 now measures what the camera actually sends. G1 passes if the sub stream is <=300 kbps at >=640x360 and >=5 fps, the first frame arrives <=3 s after a pre-connected stream is opened, the relay location is acceptable (H-14), and Katch events arrive within 30 s. See docs/vendor/warner-h5sdk.md.
